The Stone Gods

by Jeanette Winterson

Quotes

  1. “Maybe, baby, that promised land was there and I missed it. Look at it glittering in the light. But the truth is I am inventing the maybe. I can only make the choices I make, so why torture myself with what I might have done, when all I can handle is what I have done? The Maybe Islands are hostile to human life.” (102)
  2. “There will be men and women, there will be fire. There will be settlements, there will be wars. There will be planting and harvest, music and dancing. Someone will make a painting in a cave, someone will make a statue and call it God. Someone will see you and call your name. Someone will hold you, dying, across his knees.” (111)
  3. “And I thought of the immense human effort that is put to enterprises of all kinds that yield no obvious return and yet are deemed more worthy than the grazing of oxen and the manufacture of cloth. I do not deem this a fault. There must be some part of Man that is more than his daily round.” (131)

Thoughts

  • My least favorite Jeanette Winterson book so far
  • Not bad by any means, the writing was still beautiful, but it kind of felt like it didn’t have quite the same spark as her earlier works in a way that’s hard to explain?
    • Sort of exploring similar themes – love, history, free will – in similar ways, but just going through the motions
  • The scifi aspect just didn’t work for me
    • It felt sort of heavy handed in its messaging
  • The characters were flat, and Billie often felt like a mouthpiece for the author
    • She didn’t feel like a product of her time and place
    • Like for some reason she was the only one who could see through the facade of her world and everyone else was just mindlessly buying into it – but why?
    • The side characters felt more like props for social commentary than actual people
  • I do think it gets better after the first section, when the connections start coming together, but it never fully manages to overcome these issues
