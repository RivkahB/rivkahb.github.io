About

My name is Rivkah Bryski and I am an MLIS student at Pratt Institute. This website was created for my Information Technologies (INFO 654) class. It is a sample from my 2024 reading journal, which I've been keeping since 2019 in various formats.

I decided to start keeping a reading journal as a way to keep track of the books I read, to think more deeply about what I was reading, and to practice writing consistently in a low-stakes context. For every book I read, I record when I read it, copy down some quotes that resonated with me, and write some of my personal thoughts and analysis on the book. I find it to be a meditative way to spend some extra time with the books I read.

Every book cover image on the home page links to the journal entry for that book. The Reading Log lists all the books I read in 2024, in the order I read them. The books which have journal entries on this site are also linked to in the Reading Log. The Resources page lists the resources I used in the creation of this website.
